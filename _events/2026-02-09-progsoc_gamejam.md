GameDevSig collaborated with ProgSoc to organise a beginner-friendly gamejam! The games were judged over 5 categories, and each winning team won a prize, sponsored in collaboration between the two societies.

### Winners:
- Visuals / Art: **Timelight**
- Audio: **Good Farms**
- Theme Accuracy: **Time Tanks**
- Creativity / Uniqueness: **THEY SHALL ROT**
- Gameplay: **THEY SHALL ROT**
