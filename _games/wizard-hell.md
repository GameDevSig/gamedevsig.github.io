A Fast paced Wizard Bullet Hell!

Constantly face an escalating battle as enemies keep spawning faster!

Absorption of elements:

There are 4 bullet variants that enemies can shoot at you, each corresponding to an element. Apart from **Lightning**(Yellow), you can absorb **Wind**(green),**Water**(blue) and **Fire**(red). 

You can absorb one element of bullets at a time, and you can cycle through which element you are absorbing at anytime by pressing "**Q**" or "**E**". 

Absorbed elements: 

The elements that you stored is indicated by three symbols at the bottom of the screen.
