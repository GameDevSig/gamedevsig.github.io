Choose your mask and slash your way through hordes of monsters in order to [Feature removed due to time constraints]

Masks of Eternity is an arcade game where the mask you choose determines your combat abilities. The idea was to add trinkets and pieces to the mask in order to gain new abilities and to increase your stats, but we had to scale down to a core game with only two predefined masks.

<!-- CONTEXT -->

Submission for the 2-day long <a class="is-link" target="_blank" href="https://itch.io/jam/edjam26">EdJam26</a> (local instance of the <a class="is-link" target="_blank" href="https://globalgamejam.org/">Global Game Jam</a>), with the theme "Mask".

All game assets including music and sprites were made during the jam.
